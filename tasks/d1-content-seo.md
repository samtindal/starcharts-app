# D1 — Content & SEO

**Scope:** The traffic engine. Server-rendered pages in apps/web.

- Aspect × planet-pair pages: 5 aspects × 45 pairs = 225 cornerstone pages ("Mars square Venus: meaning, dates"); each embeds a mini-wheel showing the aspect + next occurrence dates (computed via astro-core search).
- Sign pages (12, `/signs/aries`…) and planet/point pages (12 incl. nodes, `/planets/mars`, `/planets/north-node`), plus "planet in sign" (144). Wheel behavior: sign glyphs have hover tooltips and click through to their pages; **planet glyphs are drag-only** (no tooltip/click — the drag is the product). Linking rules on the CLOCK page: aspect table rows link ONLY to their aspect page (one destination per row — keep the clock uncluttered); the desktop legend links to aspect-type hub pages (`/aspects/trine`). Planet-page links live **within content pages only**: on each aspect, sign, and planet page, the first mention of any other planet/sign/aspect links to its page. House pages deferred with the house feature (PLAN.md P3).
- Daily "sky today" page at stable URL with date archive — freshness signal.
- Aspect explanation copy also consumed by the wheel's hover tooltips and tap-to-explain panel (single content source, MDX or JSON). Tooltip = 2–3 sentence teaser + link to the full page. **Teaser text in the aspect table must be unique per planet pair**, not a generic per-type blurb — the prototype generates this from planet archetypes (`ARCHETYPE` × `PAIR_TEXT` templates); production replaces the generated text with the opening line of each pair's real page copy.
- **Internal-linking flywheel (required):** every aspect shown on the wheel and in the live aspect table links to its page at `/aspects/{a}-{type}-{b}` with descriptive anchor text ("Mars trine Pluto"). The clock is the highest-engagement page on the site; these contextual links distribute its authority to the long-tail pages and give crawlers a fresh path to every aspect page each day as the sky changes. URL slugs already established in the prototype — keep them.
- **Priorities revised per docs/competitive-strategy.md:** lead with computed aspect-EVENT date pages ("mars square venus 2026 dates", future retrograde calendars 2027–2035) — they need ephemeris computation competitors can't generate and resist AI-Overview cannibalization; enter aspect-MEANING pages via thin-competition combos (nodes, angles) since astro-seek/cafeastrology own the head terms.
- Technical: sitemap.xml, canonical URLs, JSON-LD, OG tags per page. Titles follow "X square Y — Starcharts".
- Tone: knowledgeable, tradition-framed ("in traditional astrology, this aspect signifies…"), never predictive-for-payment claims (ad policy, PLAN.md §7).
