# D1 | Content & SEO

**Scope:** The traffic engine. Server-rendered pages in apps/web.

- Aspect × planet-pair pages: 5 aspects × 45 pairs = 225 cornerstone pages ("Mars square Venus: meaning, dates"); each embeds a mini-wheel showing the aspect + next occurrence dates (computed via astro-core search).
- Sign pages (12, `/signs/aries`…) and planet/point pages (12 incl. nodes, `/planets/mars`, `/planets/north-node`), plus "planet in sign" (144). Wheel behavior: sign glyphs have hover tooltips and click through to their pages; **planet glyphs are drag-only** (no tooltip/click, the drag is the product). Linking rules on the CLOCK page: aspect table rows link ONLY to their aspect page (one destination per row, keep the clock uncluttered); the desktop legend links to aspect-type hub pages (`/aspects/trine`). Planet-page links live **within content pages only**: on each aspect, sign, and planet page, the first mention of any other planet/sign/aspect links to its page. House pages deferred with the house feature (PLAN.md P3).
- Daily "sky today" page at stable URL with date archive, freshness signal.
- Aspect explanation copy also consumed by the wheel's hover tooltips and tap-to-explain panel (single content source, MDX or JSON). Tooltip = 2–3 sentence teaser + link to the full page. **Teaser text in the aspect table must be unique per planet pair**, not a generic per-type blurb, the prototype generates this from planet archetypes (`ARCHETYPE` × `PAIR_TEXT` templates); production replaces the generated text with the opening line of each pair's real page copy. **NOTE 2026-07-08: this replacement was never done, the pages shipped with the templated `pairText`. Deepening them (and the thin sign/planet pages) is now owned by D5 (`d5-content-depth.md`); the composed opener written there becomes this single-source teaser.** **Closed 2026-07-13 (D5): the aspect pages, the wheel's aspect table, and the planet pages all now call `composeAspectTeaser` from `interpret.ts`; the templated `pairText`/`PAIR_TEMPLATE` were deleted from `lib/content.ts`.**
- **Internal-linking flywheel (required):** every aspect shown on the wheel and in the live aspect table links to its page at `/aspects/{a}-{type}-{b}` with descriptive anchor text ("Mars trine Pluto"). The clock is the highest-engagement page on the site; these contextual links distribute its authority to the long-tail pages and give crawlers a fresh path to every aspect page each day as the sky changes. URL slugs already established in the prototype, keep them.
- **Content-page link rail (required):** every content page carries a structured "related" rail below the body copy, content-page to content-page links only (the CLOCK-page rule is separate: aspect-table rows still link only to their aspect page per CLAUDE.md). The rail is what turns a single search landing into pages/session, the multiplier E1 monetizes; it is spec'd here because it is a content-structure decision, and referenced from `e1-growth-ads.md` §"Session depth". Per template:
  - **Aspect pair page** (`/aspects/mars-trine-pluto`): the reverse pair (`pluto-trine-mars`), the other four aspects for the same planet pair (Mars conjunct/sextile/square/opposition Pluto), both planet pages, the two signs the pair currently occupies, the aspect-type hub (`/aspects/trine`), 2 to 3 related pairs, and "aspects active now" on the live clock.
  - **Sign page** (`/signs/aries`): the ruling planet page, the same-element and same-modality sibling signs, each point-in-sign (already the chip row), the twelve-signs hub, and the live clock.
  - **Planet page** (`/planets/mars`): the sign(s) the planet rules, the planet-in-sign page for the sign it currently occupies (plus the full planet-in-sign set), the aspects the planet is currently making (to their aspect pages), 2 to 3 related points, and the live clock.
  - **Planet-in-sign page** (`/planets/mars/aries`): the parent planet page and sign page, the same planet in the adjacent signs (prev/next) plus the full set, the sign's other points, the planet's current aspects, and the live clock.
  - First-mention rule still applies inside the body copy (D1 above): the first mention of any other point/sign/aspect links to its page; the rail is additive, not a replacement. Keep anchors descriptive.
  - **Houses are deferred to P3** (PLAN.md P3); no house pages exist in v1, so no house links are in scope until the house feature ships. When it does, extend the rail (sign to its natural house, planet to the house it rules) as part of that work.
- **Priorities revised per docs/competitive-strategy.md:** lead with computed aspect-EVENT date pages ("mars square venus 2026 dates", future retrograde calendars 2027–2035), they need ephemeris computation competitors can't generate and resist AI-Overview cannibalization; enter aspect-MEANING pages via thin-competition combos (nodes, angles) since astro-seek/cafeastrology own the head terms.
- Technical: sitemap.xml, canonical URLs, JSON-LD, OG tags per page. Titles follow "X square Y, Starcharts".
- Tone: knowledgeable, tradition-framed ("in traditional astrology, this aspect signifies…"), never predictive-for-payment claims (ad policy, PLAN.md §7).
